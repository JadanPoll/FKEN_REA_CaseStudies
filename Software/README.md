Within each folder, `investigation.json` owns ordinary-language entry questions (`curiosity_atlas`), conditional navigation (`navigation_bridges`), canonical technical `nodes`, reusable question-set projections (`landscape_views`), qualifications and optional HTMlets. `glossary.json` owns permanent concept IDs, labels, aliases, categories and meaningful non-equivalent relationships. Vocabulary can expand independently of the question landscape; never require a question for each term. `evidence.json` owns replayable JSON plans, run IDs, compact result receipts, source filenames, SHA-256 digests and half-open UTF-16 ranges. Keep stable study IDs and append new evidence rather than overwrite older observations. Reconstructed plans are explicitly marked; fresh deployments may differ.

The software-level [investigation.json](investigation.json) is only a product navigation index, with optional cross-product mechanism references. The root [rsm-view.json](../rsm-view.json) registers the index and product views in the canonical viewer. Neither duplicates educational content. Add a new product folder and its view/index entry when a new product is investigated.

Start from a question, inspect the mechanism, then expand **Investigation plans & receipts** to recover and copy the relevant JSON. Use the retained archive if available; verify digests before treating old offsets as evidence. A successful plan is not necessarily a successful hypothesis test.

## Discovery and semantic persistence

Reuse the [Kami glossary contract](https://github.com/JadanPoll/Kami_Uploads_RSM_Tree/blob/main/GLOSSARY_SCHEMA.md), [question classification](https://github.com/JadanPoll/Kami_Uploads_RSM_Tree/blob/main/QUESTION_TYPES.md) and [authoring discipline](https://github.com/JadanPoll/Kami_Uploads_RSM_Tree/blob/main/AGENTS.md). Treat these as the existing architecture, not templates to reinvent. Preserve term IDs, distinguish aliases from related concepts, and link only substantively relevant terms. A glossary entry may have no associated investigation.

AI Pill's discovery map is the upstream empirical surface: identifiers, lexical motifs, occurrence counts, distinct files/regions, and their source coordinates. Set union broadens retrieval, intersection identifies co-occurrence, and set difference surfaces newly observed or unmatched candidates. A high occurrence count is not intellectual importance; use distinct-file support, diversity and actual source inspection to interpret it. Co-occurrence is not proof of a semantic relationship. The glossary is the persistent, curated concept landscape, not a dump of mined strings. Normalize genuine synonyms, keep different concepts separate, and permit promising leads without claiming they are present or implemented. Retain the associated query and compact counts in evidence when consequential. Compare deployments only with recorded archive identities and coverage.

For a new investigation, first recover the product's existing vocabulary, questions and evidence. Expand the smallest useful surface: new concept, new ordinary question, new source evidence, or a better navigation route. No quota-driven taxonomy and no duplicating technical explanations. Test real frustration-to-resource traversal and the reverse route from an independently chosen valuable mechanism.

## Public persistence discipline

This is a curiosity-led educational collection, not an exhaustive engineering project archive. Preserve the question, investigation plan, observation, interpretation, source identity and limits at the resolution needed to understand or reuse the mechanism. Pursue deeper collection only when a consequential question requires it. Do not republish private reports, user data, session identifiers, credentials or proprietary source bundles. Run IDs here identify local AI Pill investigation receipts, not service sessions. If original evidence cannot be recovered, say so rather than promote the summary into independent certification.

Source-inspected is distinct from runtime-observed, and both are distinct from a demonstrated transferable implementation. New questions can be added without demanding immediate answers. Extend a canonical record as evidence changes; preserve old receipts and stable IDs. Prefer distinct mechanisms over many superficially different examples.

## HTMlets

Optional node field: `htmlet: {title, height, html}`. HTML is a small educational fragment, with local interaction script if useful. The viewer supplies shared typography, controls, metrics and tile styling; the fragment is rendered on demand inside a sandboxed iframe without same-origin access, with restrictive CSP. No external dependency or live application interaction is needed. These examples are conceptual models, not performance measurements or exact reproductions. Keep them small and do not create a new shared primitive unless repeated educational use justifies it.

## Current educational population and retrieval audit

Claude has 9 chapters, 7 source-inspected studies and 9 open directions; Discord has 8 chapters, 5 source-inspected studies and 9 open directions. The glossaries have 109 and 101 stable concepts respectively, grouped by chapter, with relevant concepts shared across chapter routes by ID. Definitions are retrieval aids, not new implementation claims. The 18 new directions include observed lexical leads and explicitly prospective research; their proposed plans have not run. Old inspected studies and HTMlets remain intact.

The viewer's opt-in `search_profile: "descriptive"` ranks meaningful tokens, titles, aliases, concept descriptions and one-hop relationships; vocabulary search can also recover the neighborhood of a relevant question. Exact concepts outrank weaker matches. Chapter cards distinguish inspected and open records, and chapter inspectors expose their vocabulary. Existing Kami collections retain their previous search profile.

For a compact regression audit, the previous implementation returned intended existing destinations for 11 of 19 descriptive searches. The expanded, tuned set now gives 31/31 top-three question scores. A 25-query diagnostic set gives 25/25 after vocabulary revisions; a further 12-query set gives 10/12. These are author-generated probes of the actual ranking function, not independent learner experiments, and chapter/card rendering may affect actual navigation. Three unrelated-domain queries per product returned no question or vocabulary match. All 30 question projections retain their canonical targets, glossary IDs and evidence references. Browser interaction, plan execution and real learner traversability remain unverified.

Recorded source URLs and archive IDs are now preserved for previously inspected excerpts. Discovery locators retain exact filenames, hashes and search signatures; a sampled region is not an inspected implementation. Where the report cannot establish the exact original input JSON, label the replay as reconstructed. A proposed plan has no receipt. If assets have changed, reacquire current source and relocate by signature; do not reuse historical offsets blindly.

Do not optimize for term counts or tuned search scores: evaluate breadth, depth, variance, recursive fill, novelty and recoverability against [OPERATIONAL.md](../OPERATIONAL.md). Future additions should add a consequential intellectual dimension or populate an existing territory, not create near-duplicate headings.

Do not equate passed collection with a proven hypothesis, or a run's description with what its excerpts actually establish. No proprietary source excerpts or raw bulk export are republished.
